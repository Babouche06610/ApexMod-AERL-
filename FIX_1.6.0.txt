AERL 1.6.3 VELOCITY ALPHA ENGINE

- Generic cosmetic swaps remain on AERL's existing engine.
- Alpha Reward donors (Gold Rush/Goldstone) route to a standalone helper built directly from VelocityRL's current Python swap engine.
- The helper is compiled during release.ps1 with PyInstaller and included in the final AERL installer. End users do not need Python.
- AERL stages the generated package, validates output exists/is non-trivial, then atomically installs it after its normal vanilla backup.
- Thumbnails are disabled for Alpha tests to reduce crash surface.
- Restore All remains the recovery path.
