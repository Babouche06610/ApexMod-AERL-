AERL 1.0.0 — GOAL EXPLOSIONS

- Goal Explosion swaps are no longer intentionally blocked by AERL.
- Classic remains the preferred/base item where the existing Garage logic selects it.
- Selecting/applying a Goal Explosion displays a warning:
  "Goal Explosion swaps are experimental and may cause Rocket League to crash."
- User can Continue or Cancel.
- Existing file/rebuild validation remains enabled.
